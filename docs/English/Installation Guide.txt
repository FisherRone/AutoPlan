[English] Installation Guide
📥 Install the app: Drag AutoPlan.app into the Applications folder in Finder.


⚠️ If you encounter an error when running
When opening the app for the first time, you may see a pop-up like this:

“AutoPlan.app” Not OpenedApple could not verify “AutoPlan.app” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.[Done] [Move to Trash]

Reason: This happens because I have not paid for an Apple Developer membership, so Apple restricts my app.

Solution:
1. Click [Done] to close the pop-up.
2. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Security.
3. In the section that says [“AutoPlan.app” was blocked to protect your Mac.], click [Open Anyway].
4. After that, opening AutoPlan.app will no longer trigger the warning.


❓ How to use

1. After opening the app, a calendar-shaped icon will appear in the menu bar. This is the app's menu bar icon.
2. Click the menu bar icon, go to Settings, and enter your API Key.
3. Then click the menu bar icon again, then you can trigger the [Extract Schedule] or [Generate Weekly Report] functions.
